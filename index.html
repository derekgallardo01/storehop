ONE LIST. EVERY STORE.

Your shopping list shouldn't care where you buy it.

Traditional shopping-list apps organize your life around separate store lists. StoreHop does the opposite.

You maintain one master list of everything you need and tell StoreHop which stores normally carry each item. StoreHop takes care of the rest.

Need mozzarella? Add it once.

If you normally buy mozzarella at Kroger, Publix, and Walmart, it automatically appears on all three store views.

Buy it at Publix? Check it off once

and it disappears from Kroger and Walmart too.

One item. Multiple stores. One source of truth.

SHOP WHEREVER YOU HAPPEN TO BE

Never waste a trip to a store you're already in.

Ever walked out of a store only to remember that you needed something they sold? StoreHop helps prevent that.

Open the app whenever you're at a store and StoreHop shows you everything you currently need that you normally buy there.

You don't have to decide beforehand where you're going to buy the milk, toothpaste, paper towels, or dog food.

Just tell StoreHop what you need. Then buy it wherever it's most convenient.

YOUR LIST. IN THE ORDER YOU ACTUALLY SHOP.

Stop zigzagging across the store.

The produce section might be near the entrance at one store and at the back of another.

StoreHop lets you arrange categories independently for every store, so your shopping list follows the way you actually walk through that location.

Put Produce first at one store, Bakery first at another, and Pharmacy last at a third. Your items are automatically grouped into those categories, giving you an efficient, aisle-aware shopping list wherever you shop.

Less backtracking. Less wandering. Faster shopping.
